## 3. Frozen POSTs (do not change shapes)

Client already exists in `apps/web/lib/api.ts`:

| Action | Call | Body |
|---|---|---|
| Fund | `api.mayorFund` | `{ amountUsdc }` — 6-dec **string**. `"5000000"` = 5 USDC. Convert with `parseUsdc()` from `lib/usdc.ts`. Never JS floats on the wire. |
| Loan | `api.mayorLoanDecision` | `{ loanId: string, approve: boolean }` |
| Rate | `api.mayorRate` | `{ bps: number }` integer **100–2000** (`BASE_RATE_MIN_BPS` / `BASE_RATE_MAX_BPS` in `@agent-town/shared`) |

Success: `{ txHash, explorerUrl }`. Failure: `ApiRequestError` with `status`, `code`, `message`.

- Mock: POSTs always succeed (fake hash + `https://testnet.arcscan.app/tx/…`). No `ALLOW_BROADCAST` needed.
- Real (`API_MODE=real`): **501** `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` unless `ALLOW_BROADCAST=true`. Surface that as an error toast. Do not fabricate a tx.
